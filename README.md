# 60-Minute UI/UX Design Challenge: Multimedia Solutions
### The Concept

A rapid, 60-minute design and development sprint to modernize the digital identity of Multimedia Solutions, a premier agency with 33 years of experience.

### The Strategy

Timeframe: 1 Hour (Concept to Code).

### Workflow:

AI-integrated design process to accelerate high-fidelity prototyping.

### Visual Identity:

Shifted to a premium "Gold Partner" aesthetic using Syne and DM Sans typography.

### UX Focus: 
Reduced cognitive load by simplifying the service grid and highlighting core authority metrics.

### Technical Skills Demonstrated
- Professional Experience: Backed by 3+ years of global collaboration with teams like Wild Me, Curi, and New Age.
- Research-Led: Applied experience in user interviews and stakeholder research to drive layout decisions.
- Frontend Execution: Developed directly in HTML5/CSS3 to ensure technical feasibility and a clean handoff for engineering.
